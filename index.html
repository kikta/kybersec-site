Services

Independent advice during ransomware and extortion events
When an attack is underway, I advise executives, boards, and investors as an independent voice outside the incident response and negotiation firms. That includes a second opinion on how those firms are handling the event, alternate analysis of the threat actor and its likely behavior, and modeling of response scenarios so leadership can decide with a clear view of the tradeoffs.
Strategic threat analysis
Assessment of political, physical, and cyber threats to a company, its platform, its supply chain, and its people, including state-backed threats to personnel in high-risk jurisdictions and executive travel into environments with elevated risk of device compromise and technical surveillance. This is the work I did for Twitter’s security leadership.
Corporate AI adoption and security
Practical guidance on putting AI to work across the enterprise: where it creates real value, how to secure models, data, and agents, and what governance looks like in operation rather than on paper. Drawn from running AI governance and deployment across every function of a security company.
High-assurance security architecture
Feasibility, cost, and design for environments that must hold against the most capable state-backed threats, including frontier AI labs.
Federal and defense cyber strategy
Advice for companies on positioning, partnerships, and capability for government cyber work.
Briefings
Executive and board briefings on the threat landscape and cyber policy.

About

Jason Kikta served more than 23 years in the U.S. Marine Corps, including seven at U.S. Cyber Command’s Cyber National Mission Force, where he founded the private-sector partnership program, led election defense for 2018 and 2020, built Hunt Forward, and ran the public threat-disclosure program.

He was strategic threat consultant to Twitter’s CSO and CISO and spent four years as CISO of Automox, adding the CTO role in 2025. He teaches cyber espionage and statecraft at the Johns Hopkins SAIS Alperovitch Institute and advises on AI, cyber, and ransomware policy at the Institute for Security and Technology.
